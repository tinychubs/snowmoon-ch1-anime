# City multiview reference prompts: Meldan, Kalimar district

Generate the master sheet first, then each panel, and reuse them as reference images for every environment shot.

## Style lock

```text
Modern Japanese anime film style, clean line art, soft cel shading, painterly nature backgrounds, luminous natural daylight, gentle color grading, lush green and warm stone palette with deep purple accents, high detail, cinematic 16:9 framing, consistent character design across shots, no grime, no dust, no text artifacts.
```

## Panels

### A. Aerial establishing (used in Scene 1)

```text
High angle wide shot over the tree canopy of Kalimar district, Meldan. Stone-brick houses hidden among trees, curling foot paths, distant mountain with tall castle-tower apartments, a sky bridge crossing a road in the mid distance. Soft morning haze, drifting leaves. Anime film style.
```

### B. Forest footpath (Scenes 1 to 4)

```text
Eye-level view down a foot path with tall trees on both sides. Large clean stone-brick medieval-style houses behind the trees, small signs for clothing, flowers and food. Side sidewalks branching left and right with a bench on the right side. Dappled sunlight.
```

### C. Sky bridge (Scene 5)

```text
Smooth sky bridge rising from a green foot path, crossing over a busy road with cars, autobuses and delivery vehicles. Cool breeze feeling. View along the road for about two kilometers that turns right, and a forested mountain with castle-tower apartments beyond.
```

### D. Apartment street with soundproof barrier (Scenes 6 to 7)

```text
Street with a row of five-storey blue-grey apartment buildings on the left, walking paths and small shops between them, tall trees shading the sidewalk. On the right, a tall soundproof barrier painted green and blue, partly hidden behind a thick row of trees. Distant aeroplane-shaped corporate headquarters visible between the trees.
```

### E. Concert entrance and field (Scenes 7 to 14)

```text
Opening in the green-blue soundproof barrier with a glowing green circle scanner on the gate. Beyond it, a large open grass field enclosed by the barrier, a stage with red and orange lights, a crowd of dancers and chatters wearing band shirts, with a few people in dark purple hooded robes. Fire exit door on the right side.
```

### F. Autobus interior (Scenes 14 to 15)

```text
Interior of a clean, nearly empty local autobus, natural light through big windows, two seats on the left with robed passengers, a teenager with a backpack on the right. Two advertisement posters on the walls: one about lifelong learning showing a child, a middle-aged woman and an elderly man reading textbooks together; one for a drink brand with a yellow-and-green bottle.
```

### G. Family house exterior and interior (Scenes 16 to 20)

```text
Warm evening exterior of a modest stone-and-wood family home with trees, then interior: stairs, a large dining table, a couch, a bathroom door, cozy lamp light. Clean lived-in feel with personal items: shoes by the door, tablets, a compute box on the table.
```

### H. Signature details (insert references)

```text
Detail sheet: stone brick texture, moss and leaves growing into walls, hanging shop signs, barrier paint pattern in green and blue, the glowing green gate scanner, the autobus body with rounded front, small delivery vehicle, the sky bridge railing.
```
