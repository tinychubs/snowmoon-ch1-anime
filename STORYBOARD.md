## Scene 01: Meldan establishing shot
**Time:** 0:00 - 0:10  
**Location:** Forest footpath, Kalimar district, Meldan  
**Assets needed:** City panels A and B. Gladias (tiny, distant, hood down) in the last beat.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-4s**: Slow crane-down from above the tree canopy to reveal clean stone-brick houses merged into trees and plants. Drifting leaves. Title card fades in and out: Meldan, Veridia - 3724 Snowmoon 3.
- **4-8s**: Gentle push-in along the foot path. Small signs for clothing, flowers and food. Nature and buildings feel like one thing.
- **8-10s**: A tiny figure in a dark purple robe walks into frame under the trees, hood down. Hold.

**Narration N1:** 三七二四年、雪月の三日。ヴェリディアの首都メルダンは、森のように静かに息づいていた。  
*Year 3724, the third day of Snowmoon. Meldan, the capital of Veridia, breathed as quietly as a forest.*

**Audio / SFX:** Soft forest ambience, distant birdsong, light wind. Gentle acoustic guitar and piano theme starts quietly.

## Scene 02: Gladias walks, Emerald audio plays
**Time:** 0:10 - 0:20  
**Location:** Forest footpath  
**Assets needed:** Gladias (everyday outfit + purple robe, hood down), neck band, earpiece, hand device. City panel B.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Side tracking shot of Gladias walking calmly between tall trees, robe swaying.
- **3-6s**: Close-up: he breathes in, eyes closing briefly, lips curving slightly. Earpiece and silk neck band visible.
- **6-10s**: Insert on the hand device in his palm: soft emerald orb pulses with an audio waveform. Behind him, houses dissolve into plants in soft focus.

**Emerald (AI)** (voice over, faint): …空気感染の仕組みと、換気や密度が街全体の感染者数に与える影響の計算法をご説明します  
*...Let me explain how airborne infection spreads, and how to calculate what ventilation and density do to citywide case counts.*

**Audio / SFX:** Birdsong, footsteps on soft ground. Emerald voice sits softly in the mix like a podcast in one earpiece.

## Scene 03: Drone flyover and watch safety check
**Time:** 0:20 - 0:30  
**Location:** Forest footpath  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, the toy drone (prop sheet), watch UI. City panel B.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-2s**: Small toy drone glides fast over his head from behind, its shadow crossing him. He looks up, startled.
- **2-5s**: Macro close-up on his wrist watch: a green check lights up. Three short UI lines slide in: Toy drone verified / Keeps a safe distance from people / Nothing else shared.
- **5-8s**: Follow the drone as it curves off along a left sidewalk. Clear look at its ball shape with feather-like rotors on three sides.
- **8-10s**: Back to Gladias, relaxed and smiling, lowering his wrist.

**Narration N2:** この街では、おもちゃのドローンでさえ、無害であることを証明する。そして、それ以外は何も明かさない。  
*In this city even a toy drone proves that it is harmless, and reveals nothing else.*

**Audio / SFX:** Soft rotor whirr with a whoosh, gentle confirmation chime for the watch, light ambience continues.

## Scene 04: Boy catches the drone, bench kindness
**Time:** 0:30 - 0:40  
**Location:** Side sidewalks off the footpath  
**Assets needed:** Drone boy, two small siblings on tricycles, grandmother + small child, seated couple, the toy drone. City panel B.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Low angle: the drone zips overhead and the boy jumps and snatches it out of the air with one hand. Two younger siblings on tricycles behind him.
- **3-5s**: He holds it up and shouts in triumph.
- **5-10s**: Pan right to another sidewalk. A grandmother with a small child approaches a bench. A seated couple stands up and offers their seats. The grandmother bows her thanks and sits down.

**Drone boy** (shouting): よっしゃー！ぼくの五ポイント！  
*Yes! Five points for me!*

**Grandmother** (warm): ありがとうねえ、ご親切に  
*Thank you, dear, how kind.*

**Audio / SFX:** Kids laughing, tricycle bells, a happy woodwind motif for the boy, soft warm strings for the bench moment.

## Scene 05: Sky bridge and the whispered Pause
**Time:** 0:40 - 0:50  
**Location:** Sky bridge over a busy road  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, neck band, city panel C.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: The foot path curves upward and smoothly becomes a sky bridge over a busy road with cars, autobuses and delivery vehicles.
- **3-6s**: Gladias steps to the middle; a chilly breeze blows hair and robe into his face.
- **6-8s**: Extreme close-up of his neck band and throat: his lips barely move, only the throat, as he whispers.
- **8-10s**: Reverse wide: the road runs two kilometers then turns right; a forested mountain with castle-tower apartments beyond.

**Narration N3:** 今日、グラディアスに求められているのは、人間の世界について意見を述べることだった。  
*Today, Gladias was asked to give his opinion on the human world.*

**Gladias** (whisper, silent-speech): 一時停止。この景色を、少し味わいたいんだ  
*Pause. I want to take in this view for a moment.*

**Audio / SFX:** Wind rush, traffic hum from below, Emerald audio cuts out on the whisper. Music opens up with a wide orchestral swell.

## Scene 06: The vote request: Badra St #1103
**Time:** 0:50 - 1:00  
**Location:** Street beside the soundproof barrier  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, hand device UI (vote screen), city panel D.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Gladias walks beside a tall green-and-blue soundproof barrier hidden behind trees. His device buzzes; he pulls it out.
- **3-6s**: UI insert: Vote on Badra St #1103 / Five-storey apartment building, blue-grey / slider from -5 to 5 with a Select button.
- **6-8s**: Reverse: the blue-grey five-storey building with tree-lined foot paths, in soft afternoon light.
- **8-10s**: Tax card slides in: Total land tax 1.51%. Quick cut to a tiny aeroplane-shaped headquarters in the far distance, its tax card reading 1.89%.

**Narration N4:** 市民はくじで選ばれ、建物の美しさに投票する。その評価が、建物の税を決めるのだ。  
*Citizens are chosen by lot to vote on how beautiful a building is, and that score decides its tax.*

**Audio / SFX:** Device buzz, soft UI clicks, city hum. Light curious pizzicato motif.

## Scene 07: Slider vote and the concert gate
**Time:** 1:00 - 1:10  
**Location:** Street to the concert entrance  
**Assets needed:** Gladias (robe, hood up at the end), device UI, gate scanner, city panel E.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Gladias zooms his device at the aeroplane building, smirks, and shakes his head with amusement.
- **3-5s**: Slider close-up: his thumb nudges it only slightly to the right, then taps Select.
- **5-8s**: He arrives at an opening in the barrier and holds his device to a glowing green circle on the gate.
- **8-10s**: Gate lights turn green; he pulls his hood up and walks through as music pours from inside.

**Gladias** (thought): あの飛行機ビルの税は…まあ、妥当だな  
*That aeroplane building's tax... well, it's fair enough.*

**Gate machine** (flat voice): 認証しました。ようこそ  
*Verified. Welcome.*

**Audio / SFX:** Muffled bass from the concert growing louder, gate confirmation beep, soft crowd noise.

**Assets needed:** Gladias in the robe (hood up), crowd extras, stage, singer (Dreadknot). City panel E.

## Scene 09: Tax rubric and the debate
**Time:** 1:20 - 1:30  
**Location:** Front of the concert crowd  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, rubric UI (Tiers 1 to 5), crowd, stage.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Device screen: a simplified rubric table of Tiers 1 to 5 with tax rates from 0% to 40%.
- **3-7s**: Cross-cut: cheering, dancing crowd, then the stage. Floating words appear over the scene: Advocating vs Showcasing.
- **7-10s**: Gladias looks uneasy: a brief flash of stern authority figures (silhouetted Sentinels) preferring a softer society, then back to his face.

**Narration N5:** グラディアスは舵取り会の見習い。彼の判断は、今まさに採点されている。  
*Gladias is an apprentice of the Order of Steering, and his judgment was being scored right at that moment.*

**Gladias** (thought): 暴力を煽っているのか、それとも見せ物にしているだけなのか…  
*Are they inciting violence, or just putting it on as a show...?*

**Audio / SFX:** Concert muffled slightly as Gladias concentrates, his thought line and narrator sit above the mix.

## Scene 10: Prediction score and the Tier 3 vote
**Time:** 1:30 - 1:40  
**Location:** Front of the concert crowd  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, device UI (score gauge, Acolyte chat, tier buttons).

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Score gauge fills to 92 with a bold line at 90: the deadline for becoming a Sentinel.
- **3-6s**: Chat panel: two Acolytes argue, one for Tier 3 and one for Tier 4. Small floating numbers and fractions orbit Gladias's head as he computes his odds.
- **6-8s**: The band finishes a song; the crowd roars. He looks at the joyful faces and steels himself.
- **8-10s**: His thumb taps Tier 3. A brief, proud smile.

**Gladias** (thought): よし、賭けてみよう。ティア3だ  
*Alright, I'll take the gamble. Tier 3.*

**Audio / SFX:** Heartbeat-like bass pulse under the crowd, a cymbal swell, and a quiet UI click on the vote.

## Scene 11: "It's an Order member!"

**Visual guidance**

*Hey, everyone! There's an Order member here!*

## Scene 12: Burly man and the Arctic Emperor trick
**Time:** 1:50 - 2:00  
**Location:** Concert crowd  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, burly man, crowd.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Gladias turns to run and finds a large burly man pointing a hand device straight at his face.
- **3-6s**: Slow push-in on Gladias's eyes. His expression shifts from panic to a schoolboy's mischievous glint.
- **6-8s**: He snaps his head and points off-screen with fake excitement.
- **8-10s**: The man turns to look, confused.

**Narration N7:** 思慮深い哲学者の仮面が外れ、いたずら好きの少年が顔を出した。  
*The thoughtful philosopher's mask slipped, and a mischievous boy showed his face.*

**Gladias** (excited, fake): 見ろ！北極皇帝の新しい声明じゃないか？  
*Look! Isn't that a new statement from the Arctic Emperor?*

**Audio / SFX:** Music hard-cuts to silence for the push-in, then a small comic sting on the trick.

## Scene 13: Snatch, delete, throw
**Time:** 2:00 - 2:10  
**Location:** Concert crowd  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, burly man, snatched hand device, device UI.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-2s**: Gladias's hand snatches the device from the man's arm.
- **2-5s**: He spins away and barks a command at the stolen device.
- **5-7s**: Device screen: it deletes files and briefly shows thumbnails, one of them is Gladias's face at bottom right. Screen goes black.
- **7-10s**: He throws the device high in the air. The burly man's eyes and hands follow it upward.

**Gladias** (quick, commanding): 直近一時間の音声・画像・動画をすべて削除。再起動  
*Delete all audio, images and video from the last hour. Reboot.*

**Stolen device** (flat voice): 四件のファイルを削除しました  
*Four files deleted.*

**Audio / SFX:** Fast percussion hits, device blip, a rising whoosh for the throw.

## Scene 20: The anonymous message
**Time:** 3:10 - 3:20  
**Location:** Family dining table  
**Assets needed:** Gladias, hand device (message card), family in the background.

**Visual guidance**

- **0-3s**: Warm shot of the family eating together. Soft laughter.
- **3-6s**: Gladias's device buzzes on the table. He picks it up and the smile fades.
- **6-8s**: Extreme close-up on the screen: a message card appears from Anonymous with a verified reputation badge, followed by a second message.
- **8-10s**: Zoom into his stunned eyes as the music cuts out. Hard cut to black.

**Narration N10:** そのとき、彼の秘密を知る誰かが、静かに連絡をよこした。  
*And then someone who knew his secret quietly reached out.*

**On-screen message 1** (text only): コンサートで見かけました。あなたが会の人間だと知っています  
*I saw you at the concert. I know you are in the Order.*

**On-screen message 2** (text only): 通報して賞金を得るつもりはありません。ただ、お願いがあります  
*I will not report you for the bounty. But I have a request.*

**Audio / SFX:** Warm music slowly thins, device buzz, a low drone, then silence at the cut to black.
