# Character and prop reference prompts

Each sheet is generated as one image and then used as the reference image for every clip that features the character. Add the shared layout block at the start of each prompt.

## Shared layout block

```text
Character reference sheet, modern Japanese anime film style, clean line art, soft cel shading, plain light grey background, 16:9. Layout: full-body turnaround on the left (front, three-quarter, side, back), five head-and-shoulder expressions on the right (neutral, smiling, surprised, worried, mischievous), color palette swatches along the bottom, small labelled prop callouts.
```

## 4.1 Gladias (main, Acolyte of the Order)

Adult man in his early forties. Slim, average height. Calm, thoughtful, gentle humor. Two outfits: everyday and the Veridian Privacy Robe.

```text
CHARACTER: Gladias, adult man in his early forties, slim build, short dark tousled hair, calm thoughtful dark eyes, gentle half-smile. OUTFIT A (everyday): soft muted green linen tunic, dark trousers, simple brown shoes. A thin silken cloth band wrapped tightly around his neck (a silent throat microphone), a small wireless earpiece in one ear, a smartwatch on his left wrist, a palm-sized hand device. OUTFIT B (Veridian Privacy Robe): loose-fitting dark purple robe covering the whole body from hood to ankles, matching dark purple shoes with high heels and deliberately uneven soles, a strap-belt face cover that tightens across the face just below the nose. Show the robe with hood up and hood down. Expression set includes a quiet contented smile and a mischievous grin.
```

## 4.2 Seila (Gladias's wife, blogger)

Adult woman in her late thirties. Warm, curious, practical. Writes a blog and travels.

```text
CHARACTER: Seila, adult woman in her late thirties, warm brown eyes, shoulder-length wavy dark hair tied loosely at the back, slight smile lines, kind relaxed expression. OUTFIT: deep green knit cardigan over a cream top, comfortable dark trousers, soft indoor shoes. Holds a palm-sized hand device. Expressions include gentle concern, soft laughter, and a caring hug pose.
```

## 4.3 Zven (their younger son)

Boy around 8. Small and bouncy, always cheerful, shouts when excited.

```text
CHARACTER: Zven, boy around 8 years old, small and energetic, messy short brown hair, big bright eyes, wide gap-toothed grin. OUTFIT: soft orange hoodie with a small star patch, khaki shorts, white sneakers. Add extra poses: jumping down stairs three steps at a time, running into a hug, cheering with both arms up.
```

## 4.4 Febric (co-family teenage boy)

Boy around 15. Tall, relaxed, dry sense of humor. Always carries a device on a cable.

```text
CHARACTER: Febric, teenage boy around 15, tall and lanky, dark curly hair, slightly sleepy calm eyes, relaxed posture. OUTFIT: grey hoodie with sleeves pushed up, dark jeans, worn sneakers. PROP: a hand device connected by a thin cable to a larger rugged compute box carried in the other hand. Expressions: laid-back, quietly amused, fondly serious.
```

## 4.5 Lily (Gladias and Seila's daughter)

Girl around 12 or 13. Quiet, withdrawn after a bad exam, absorbed in a mobile game.

```text
CHARACTER: Lily, girl around 12 to 13 years old, slim, straight dark-brown hair partly covering her face, tired downcast eyes. OUTFIT: oversized lavender hoodie, grey leggings, socks. PROP: hand device held close to her chest. Expressions: withdrawn, intensely focused on a game, a tiny softened smile, turning her face away.
```

## 4.6 The drone boy (catches the drone)

Boy around 9 or 10. Athletic, proud, part of a family with four children. Must look clearly different from Zven.

```text
CHARACTER: sporty boy around 9 to 10 years old, athletic build, short spiky light-brown hair, a small confident smirk. OUTFIT: blue and white sports jersey with the number 5, knee-length blue shorts, red sneakers, a yellow wristband. Add a mid-jump pose catching a ball-shaped drone with one hand, and a triumphant pose holding it up. Add small secondary references of two younger siblings riding tricycles (a little sister in pink, a little brother in green).
```

## 4.7 The toy drone

Small ball-shaped toy drone with feather-like rotors on three sides. Harmless, friendly, safe-looking.

```text
PROP SHEET: toy drone, small ball-shaped body about the size of a grapefruit, smooth matte pastel mint shell, three feather-like soft rotors arranged around three sides, tiny soft green status light on the front, rounded friendly design. Views: front, side, top, bottom, rotor detail close-up, size comparison next to a boy's hand, in-flight pose with soft rotor motion blur, and a "caught in hand" pose.
```
