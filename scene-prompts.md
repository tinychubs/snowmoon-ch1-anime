# Scene video prompts (base prompts)

One prompt per 10-second scene, written to be pasted into a video generator together with the character and city reference images. These are the base prompts from the production brief; per-tool adjustments were made during generation.

Modern Japanese anime film style, soft cel shading, painterly backgrounds, 16:9, 10 seconds.
